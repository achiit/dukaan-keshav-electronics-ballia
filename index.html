About the shop

One stop for power and home comforts

We are a local shop at Reti Bus Stand on Bairia Road. People come to us for batteries and inverters when the power goes, and for coolers, fridges and washing machines when the season turns.

We also keep beds and wardrobes, so a family setting up or upgrading a home can see many things in one visit instead of going from shop to shop.

You do not need to know the exact model. Tell us what you want to run or what space you have, and we will show you what we have and tell you the price plainly.
